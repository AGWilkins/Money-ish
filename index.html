Where did this come from?

Money-ish grew out of a simple idea from Clarity Cycle : before trying to change a behaviour, notice it properly.

Most money apps want access to your bank account. Money-ish wants access to your attention.

There are no scores, streaks, guilt trips or “financial health” percentages here. Just small prompts that make you look at spending, value, habits and assumptions from a slightly different angle.

Sometimes you may spend less. Sometimes you may spend more. The point is that you noticed.
